# LOY-114: Gold tier at checkout

A Gold member must feel rewarded every time they order.

## Rules from the product owner

- A member reaches Gold once they spend 500 euros in one calendar year.
- A Gold member gets free delivery on every order.
- A member keeps Gold until the end of the following calendar year.

## Existing delivery policy, unchanged by this ticket

- An order below 25 euros is charged the standard delivery fee of 4.95 euros.
- An order of 25 euros or more is delivered free of charge.

## Note from refinement

Nobody in the room was sure whether the order that takes a member past 500 euros
already counts as a Gold order.
